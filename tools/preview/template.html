Construction sequence

    Delivered to the console, over the whole stage

      The player-built example is not part of the generator: it shows what a player can build off it. Fittings stand where a player has put them, and pipe runs are strung between them and the ports as curved tubes. From the east large port a main runs level to a stand, on to a lower stand off to one side, with a branch to a third and an ordinary pipe dropping from the fitting. A distributor on the branch stand splits the main into four pipes that carry on level; a drop distributor on the lower stand sends four runs down to fittings on the ground, and a main runs level from it to a steam hub tower, whose legs taper in under a fitting; the hub hangs inside on a dropped main, three blocks off the ground. From a base port, ordinary runs go along the ground and up to a fitting overhead; heating pipes lead off along the ground into a tent of rods and cloth, with a tent stove inside whose flue goes up through the cloth. A crossover of steps and catwalk goes over the heating pipes. Past it stands a small house put up from building panels under a pitched roof. Beyond the tent a steam borer stands by its coal heap, with a bucket conveyor leading away. From another base port a pipe goes through a stop valve and a gauge station, then through a wall by a passthrough block, to a radiator standing along the far side, with underfloor grates set into the ground in front of it. Each group appears once its port has been built. To the south lies the wrecked freighter, frozen in: move over to it with W A S D.

      Drawn from the mod's own blueprint, block shapes and textures, so what shows here is what the console builds in the world. Textures are procedural placeholders.
